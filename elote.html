How matching changes electricity trade

These maps compare net regional electricity exports in 2040 with unregulated servers and with full New-Build 3P coverage. Gold marks net exporters and blue marks net importers; both panels use the same scale. Labels report annual net exports and regional demand (D), in TWh. The view shows how hourly, local matching reshapes reliance on electricity traded between regions.

Sensitivity to input assumptions

The sensitivity analysis varies one input at a time: fuel prices, technology capital costs, data-center load, and other electricity demand. It measures changes in average system cost, 2040 demand-weighted marginal electricity cost, and 2040 CO₂ emissions. Natural gas prices have the largest cost response among the assumptions tested.

Checking dispatch against network constraints

DC optimal power flow (DC OPF) tests the generation portfolios on the synthetic Texas ACTIVSg2000 network, calibrated to 2025 load and generation. The comparison tracks renewable curtailment, gas generation, operating costs, and congested lines through 2040, with an unconstrained network benchmark to isolate the effect of transmission limits.

Transmission expansion and validation

The expansion analysis adds line and transformer capacity to accommodate the modeled portfolios, then reruns hourly dispatch on the expanded network. These results show the network additions and the remaining operational effects for baseline and New-Build 3P portfolios.

Reading the results

Each region is one model zone, not a state. In comparison mode, the chart shows selected scenario − comparison scenario. A positive value means the selected scenario is higher. For net exports, positive means more electricity exported than imported.

Capacity is installed generation plus batteries; clean generation includes wind, solar, hydro, geothermal, and nuclear. Battery capacity is power capacity in GW. CO₂ is the model's regional emissions total. Values are annual outputs for the selected model period.

These are preprocessed Temoa ELOTE results for the fixed converter design and a pinned server fleet. The 50% and 100% cases apply New-Build, Hourly, Local matching to the specified share of server load. The map is a model-region approximation. Download the data CSV.
