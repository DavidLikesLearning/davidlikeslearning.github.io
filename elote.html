Checking dispatch against network constraints

DC optimal power flow tests the portfolios on the synthetic Texas ACTIVSg2000 network, tracking curtailment, gas generation, costs, and congestion.

  • Load allocation: TRE and TREW regional demand, including server load, was distributed to buses in proportion to each bus’s share of the synthetic grid’s regional load.
  • Generation siting: Regional capacity totals were preserved. Thermal, nuclear, and hydro capacity was distributed across same-fuel synthetic generator buses; existing wind, solar, and storage used EIA-860 plant locations mapped to buses. New wind and solar followed the lowest-cost supply-curve sites, connected to nearby transmission buses.
  • 2025 calibration: The ACTIVSg2000 network was updated using ERCOT 2025 load and generation and the EIA-860 fleet. Adding 10.5 GW of line and transformer rating brought modeled wind and solar curtailment to 11 TWh (5.6%), compared with a 9.8 TWh target reported for ERCOT in 2025.
  • Hourly validation: Unregulated servers and 100% 3P portfolios were each checked for 2027, 2030, 2035, and 2040 using 3,360 representative hourly slices per model year (20 weeks). These runs hold the calibrated network fixed to measure congestion as load and generation grow.

Transmission expansion and validation

Line and transformer additions accommodate the portfolios; hourly dispatch on the expanded network checks the remaining operational effects.

  • Expansion method: Starting from the calibrated 2025 grid, we expanded transmission capacity. Paired cost-minimizing linear models iteratively expanded line and transformer ratings given congested hours and then resolved annual dispatch for all hours aiming to match real curtailment (5.6%) and shedding (~0%). This expansion ran for 2027, 2030, 2035, and 2040. Upgrades carried forward to each later year.
  • Regulation impacts congestion: 100% 3P required upgrades earlier: 30 GW versus 11 GW for unregulated servers by 2027. By 2040, cumulative added ratings were both around 170 GW, excluding the initial calibration upgrades.
